Mimi: Habit Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026
The short version. Mimi works without an account, and then your habits stay on your phone (and in your own iCloud, if it is on). An account is optional: it only exists so your habits can sync between your devices. We don't show ads, we don't sell data, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

Who we are

Mimi: Habit Tracker ("Mimi", "we") is a habit tracker app developed by Emmanuela Kovalenko. Questions about privacy: vlnnns.dev@gmail.com.

Using Mimi without an account

Your habits, check-ins, notes and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is enabled on your iPhone, a copy is kept in your private iCloud storage so it can move to your other Apple devices. That copy belongs to your Apple ID; we can't see it.

Signing in

  • Sign in with Google or Apple: we receive your email address and, if you allow it, your name. With Apple's "Hide My Email" we only see a private relay address.

The daily motivation quote is generated without sending any of your data: the request contains no habits, notes or account information.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent to our servers.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your synced data and photo are protected by access rules so only your account can read or change them. On the device, your sign-in session is stored encrypted.

How long we keep data

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photo and synced data are deleted right away. Server backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within their normal backup cycle (up to 7 days).

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. You can also ask us by email; see Delete your account. Deleting the account doesn't remove habits stored on your phone unless you choose to remove them.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your data at any time. If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us at vlnnns.dev@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days.

Children

Mimi is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
