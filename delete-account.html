Mimi: Habit Tracker

Delete your account

Developer: Emmanuela Kovalenko

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Mimi: Habit Tracker.
  2. Go to Settings and tap your account at the top.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you signed in with Apple, you'll be asked to confirm with Face ID / your Apple ID once more.

Without the app

Email vlnnns.dev@gmail.com from the address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Mimi account". If you signed in with Apple using "Hide My Email", tell us that and we'll help you confirm it's your account. We'll delete the account within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address, name, sign-in method)
  • Your profile photo
  • All synced habits, check-ins, notes and settings stored with your account
  • For Apple sign-in: Mimi's access to your Apple ID is revoked

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Hosting-provider backups are overwritten within up to 7 days.
  • Habits stored on your own phone stay there unless you choose to remove them, and you can keep using Mimi without an account.
