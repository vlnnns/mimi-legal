Frequent questions

How do I add a habit?

Tap + at the bottom of the home screen, give it a name and pick the days of the week.

How do I mark a day or write a note?

Tap a circle to mark the day as done. Press and hold a circle to write a note for that day. For sport habits the note picks the icon: strength, stairs, running and more.

How do I delete a habit?

Swipe the habit to the left on the home screen and tap the trash icon.

Where are all my notes?

Open Stats and scroll to All notes, or open a habit and tap All notes for this habit.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account everything stays on your phone and works offline. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want your habits on all your devices.

How do I change the theme, colors or language?

Settings → Interface for theme, accent color, habit palette, font and text size. Settings → Language for the language.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. See also how account deletion works.
